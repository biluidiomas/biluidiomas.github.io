Poliglô – Bilíngue Idiomas Privacy Policy

Version of 2026-10-30. This policy explains what data Poliglô uses, why, who it is shared with and how you control everything. It applies in every country where the app is offered and was written to comply with the LGPD (Brazil), the GDPR (European Union and United Kingdom), US privacy laws (including California's), the APPI (Japan) and similar laws.

1. Who is responsible

The party responsible for your data (controller) is Vitor Chimello, contact: biluidiomas.contato@gmail.com. Use this contact for any privacy request.

2. Summary

  • Most of your data stays only on your device (progress, goals, settings, the encrypted AI key).
  • Your voice is only recorded when you tap the microphone, after you allow it. Recordings do not go to Poliglô's servers.
  • When you use the AI (Clara or AI correction), your audio and text go directly from your device to Google, using your key.
  • The leaderboard stores on the server only what is needed: an anonymous ID, the first name you choose and your stars.
  • We do not sell data, do not show ads and do not use advertising trackers.

3. Data that stays only on your device

This data is stored in the app's storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • lesson progress, stars, achievements, reviews and the daily plan;
  • settings, interface language, daily goals, streak and reminder times;
  • Clara's notebook (corrected points and a summary of the last conversation) and the names you gave her and yourself;
  • the AI key, encrypted (AES-256);
  • mascot customization and the background photo, if you choose one;
  • a copy of your purchases, so the app knows what is unlocked;
  • Clara's already generated voice audio (cache, up to 150 files) and downloaded voice packs.

You can delete all of this in Settings > Privacy > "Delete my data from this device", or by uninstalling the app.

4. Microphone and voice recordings

  • When it records: only when you tap the microphone button (in lessons, support material and the conversation with Clara), and only after you allow it in the app and in your device's system.
  • What for: so you can hear your own voice, compare it with the model, get a pronunciation score and talk to Clara. The app also measures on the device the duration, pauses, volume and pace of your speech to give breathing and public speaking tips.
  • Where it is kept: the recording stays in the device's memory while the screen is open, so you can listen again (🔁 button). It is discarded when you leave the screen or close the app. Poliglô does not save recordings to files and does not send recordings to Poliglô's servers.
  • When it leaves the device: only if you use the AI (Clara or AI pronunciation score). In that case the recording and the speech measurements are sent over the internet, encrypted, directly to Google's Gemini API, with your key. Without a key, the evaluation is done only on the device.
  • What we do not do: we do not use your voice to identify you (biometrics), we do not create a "voiceprint", we do not use recordings to train our own models and we do not sell them.
  • How to withdraw permission: in Settings > Privacy > Microphone, or in Android's permission settings. Without the microphone, the rest of the app keeps working.

5. The AI (Clara) and Google

  • When you use Clara or AI correction, the following is sent to Google: the recorded audio, the text you type, the recent conversation history, a short summary of previous lessons (the notebook), your first name if you provided it, and the chosen language and level.
  • It is sent with your own key, created in your Google account. Therefore Google processes this data under the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy that you accepted when creating the key. Poliglô does not receive or keep a copy of this data on any server.
  • Please note: for the free Gemini API services, Google states that it may use what is sent to improve its products and that human reviewers may read this content (rules vary by region and account type). So do not send sensitive personal data to Clara. See: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • The AI may only be used by people aged 18 or older.
  • Neural voices and lesson voices run on the device itself, without sending text or audio.

6. Leaderboard and AI reports

If you choose to appear on the leaderboard, we use the Supabase service (our processor) to store:

  • an anonymous ID generated by the app (we do not ask for email, phone or password);
  • the first name you type (no surname);
  • your stars and study days.
  • If you report one of Clara's answers: the text of that answer, the language, the app version and your anonymous ID, only to review the report and improve our filters.

If you untick "Appear on the leaderboard", your name disappears from the list. To delete your account and everything on the server, use Settings > Privacy > "Delete my leaderboard account".

7. Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. Google handles payment and your billing data under its own policy. The app only receives the purchase confirmation (product, order identifier and status) to unlock content, and keeps this confirmation on the device.

8. Downloads and technical data

To download voice packs, the app accesses file servers (such as Hugging Face and our hosting service). These servers receive the normal technical data of any connection (IP address, date and time, device type) and keep it for the time they define. We do not use this data to identify you. Google Play may also show us aggregated error and crash reports that do not identify you.

9. Reminders

Study reminders are scheduled on the device itself. No data is sent to us. You can turn them off on the goals screen or in Android's settings.

10. What we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, photos (the background photo stays on the device), advertising ID or browsing history. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell or "share" data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

11. Why we use data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract): lessons, progress, purchases and leaderboard.
  • With your consent: microphone and recordings, use of the AI and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • Legitimate interest: security, preventing leaderboard fraud and reviewing reports about AI answers, in a proportionate way.
  • Legal obligation: when the law or an authority requires it.

12. Who we share with

  • Google (Gemini API, on your account and key; Google Play for purchases and error reports).
  • Supabase (hosting of the leaderboard and AI reports), as a processor, only to make the service work.
  • File servers for voice downloads.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell, rent or trade your data.

13. International transfers

Google and Supabase may process data in other countries, such as the United States. In these cases we rely on the safeguards provided by law, such as standard contractual clauses and these providers' data protection commitments.

14. How long we keep data

  • On the device: until you delete the data or uninstall the app.
  • Recordings: only while the screen is open (in memory).
  • Leaderboard: while the account exists. Accounts unused for 24 months may be deleted. Reports about AI answers may be kept for up to 6 months after they are reviewed.
  • At Google (AI): for the time defined in Google's terms for your key.

15. Your rights

Depending on your country, you have the right to: confirm whether we process your data; access it and receive a copy; correct it; delete it; take it to another service (portability); object to processing; restrict its use; withdraw consent; and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Many of these controls are already in the app (Settings > Privacy). For the rest, write to biluidiomas.contato@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days (or within the time your country's law requires) and may ask for a simple confirmation that the account is yours.

You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country, for example: ANPD (Brazil), CNPD (Portugal), AEPD (Spain), CNIL (France), your state authority in Germany, ICO (United Kingdom), PPC (Japan), or the local authority where you live.

16. Children and teenagers

Poliglô is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child under 13 has used the app, write to biluidiomas.contato@gmail.com and we will delete the data. Between 13 and the age of digital consent in your country, use depends on a guardian's permission. The AI is for people aged 18 or older only. We always process teenagers' data in their best interest.

17. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), encryption of the AI key on the device, access rules in the leaderboard database, and we collect as little as possible. No system is 100% secure: protect your device with a password and do not share your key.

18. California and other US states

In the last 12 months, the categories of data processed were: identifiers (anonymous ID and leaderboard first name), text of reported AI answers, audio recordings (only sent to Google when you use the AI, with your key) and purchase information. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can exercise your rights by emailing biluidiomas.contato@gmail.com.

19. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way (for example, if the app starts showing ads or sending new data), we will notify you in the app and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again.
