Delete your Poliglô account

Poliglô – Bilíngue Idiomas only stores leaderboard data on the server (anonymous ID, first name, stars and study days) and any reports you make about AI answers. Everything else stays only on your device.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Poliglô > ⚙ Settings > Privacy > "Delete my leaderboard account". Everything is deleted immediately.
  • To delete what is on the device: Settings > Privacy > "Delete my data from this device", or uninstall the app.

Without the app

Email poligloidiomas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and the name shown on your leaderboard. We delete the account and all data linked to it (name, stars, study days and reports you made) within 15 days and confirm by email.

Purchases

Purchases are recorded by Google Play (not by us) and remain in your Google account even after deletion.
