Delete your Bilu account

Bilu – Bilíngue Idiomas only stores chat and leaderboard data on the server (anonymous ID, username, display name, avatar, messages, friendships, groups, stars). Everything else stays only on your device.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Bilu > ⚙ Settings > Privacy > "Delete my chat and leaderboard account". Everything is deleted immediately.
  • To delete what is on the device: Settings > Privacy > "Delete my data from this device", or uninstall the app.

Without the app

Email biluidiomas.contato@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and your chat username. We delete the account and all data linked to it (profile, messages, friendships, groups you created, leaderboard) within 15 days and confirm by email. Reports about the account may be kept for up to 6 months for safety.

Purchases

Purchases are recorded by Google Play (not by us) and remain in your Google account even after deletion.
